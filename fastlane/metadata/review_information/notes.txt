No login or demo account needed. The app works fully offline and collects no data. The UI is German.

To try it: create a pet on the welcome screen, then add a medication under "Medikamente". Time-critical medications use AlarmKit alarms (with a Live Activity countdown shortly before); stopping an alarm does not confirm the dose, only "Gabe bestätigen" does. Preventive care (deworming, tick protection) only sends notifications.

The cycle tab appears only for intact female dogs. Predictions are always shown as a range and the app states that it is not a medical device.
