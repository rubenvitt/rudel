Rudel is the health record for your dog or cat, so you never have to wonder whether today's pill was already given.

Never miss a dose: on-time reminders, real alarms if you want them, and a dose only counts once you confirm it. Deworming and tick protection get gentle notifications, and you see when supplies run low.

Ready for the vet: appointments, vaccination record and your practice in one place, plus a short PDF report before each visit.

Heat cycle for female dogs: when the next season is roughly due and which days to keep her on the leash. Always shown as a range.

Symptoms with photos and weight as a chart, so you notice changes early. Each pet gets its own photo and color.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Rudel is completely free: any number of pets, the vet report and all colors, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Rudel is not a medical device and does not replace your vet.
